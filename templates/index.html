Timing Scout

AI-Assisted FPGA Timing Report Analyzer

Upload a Vivado report_timing_summary text report and Timing Scout will extract the key timing numbers, tell you whether timing PASSED or FAILED, and give a few rule-based suggestions for what to look at next. An LLM can optionally explain the results in plain English, but it never decides PASS or FAIL and never sees your raw report.
